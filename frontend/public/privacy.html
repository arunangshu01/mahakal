Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

M.A.H.A.K.A.L. ("the app") is a personal voice AI assistant run by Arunangshu Pramanik. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how long it is kept.

What we collect

  • Google account details — if you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Your conversation — the most recent messages between you and the assistant (up to 20), so it can remember context.
  • Voice recordings — when you talk to the assistant, the recording is sent for transcription and then discarded. Recordings are not stored.
  • A session cookie (__session) — keeps you signed in and counts the free messages available to guests. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How your data is used

Only to run the assistant: to generate replies, remember your recent conversation, and show who is signed in. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Services that process your data

  • Groq — generates replies and transcribes speech.
  • Google Gemini API — backup for replies and transcription. Requests made through Google's free tier may be used by Google to improve its products.
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech — turns replies into speech.
  • Google Cloud and Firebase — hosting and the database (stored in Mumbai, India).

How long we keep it

  • Guest conversations are deleted automatically after 7 days of inactivity.
  • Signed-in conversations are deleted automatically after 90 days of inactivity.
  • You can clear your conversation history at any time.

Your choices

You can use the app as a guest without signing in, sign out at any time, and ask us to delete your account and all associated data by emailing arunangshu01@gmail.com.
